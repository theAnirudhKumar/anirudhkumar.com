02 — Approach

How a new account actually gets built out.

01

Diagnose

Read the account's real usage before the first call. Most onboarding plans get written from the sales deck instead of the product data.

02

Build

Onboarding flow, then adoption tracking, then a health score. In that order, because no health score is trustworthy without the tracking under it.

03

Instrument

Wire the score into something that surfaces risk on its own. If it lives in a spreadsheet someone has to remember to open, it doesn't exist.

04

Iterate

Revisit each quarter against what actually happened, not what the plan predicted. The plan is wrong until proven otherwise.

"They seem happy" is not a health score.
