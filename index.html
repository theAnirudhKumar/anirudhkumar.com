05 · How I work

Five things I keep coming back to.

  1. Clarity beats intelligence. The smartest idea in the room loses to the one everyone can repeat.
  2. Systems scale. People don't. If it depends on someone remembering, it's already broken.
  3. Output over activity. Busy is not a result.
  4. Speed only counts when the direction is right. Fast in the wrong direction is just far away.
  5. Adoption is the only metric. Everything else is a proxy for whether people actually use the thing.
