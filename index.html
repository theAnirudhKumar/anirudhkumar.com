02 · Work

The day job is customer success. The job inside the job is building the system.

Three times now I've joined a SaaS company where the accounts existed and the process didn't. No onboarding path, no signal for who was about to churn, no answer to "why did we lose that one" beyond a guess. Each time the work was the same: build the operating layer underneath the book, then hand it to the team.

The numbers are the part that travels. Adoption up 63% on the current book. One in ten accounts expanding 1.5 to 2 times every quarter. A $1.2M book run on infrastructure that didn't exist the month before I arrived.

06 · How I work

Five things I keep coming back to.

  1. Clarity beats intelligence. The smartest idea in the room loses to the one everyone can repeat.
  2. Systems scale. People don't. If it depends on someone remembering, it's already broken.
  3. Output over activity. Busy is not a result.
  4. Speed only counts when the direction is right. Fast in the wrong direction is just far away.
  5. Adoption is the only metric. Everything else is a proxy for whether people actually use the thing.
